# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Project Roles

These roles can be assigned as needed based on project size and complexity. One person may take on more than one role. Each project should make ownership and decision boundaries clear during planning.

### Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Guides the technical design and helps ensure the solution is maintainable, secure, and aligned with relevant architecture standards.

### Responsibilities
- Lead or coordinate technical design and document significant decisions
- Identify technical risks, quality attributes, and architecture constraints
- Advise on feasibility, effort, and technical trade-offs
- Support Developers with design questions and technical dependencies

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers on implementation choices and code quality
- Advises the Product Manager on feasibility and trade-offs without owning product priorities
- Coordinates technical risks and dependencies with the Project Manager
- Works with QA/Testing to ensure designs can be validated against quality requirements

---

### UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
Represents user needs in the design process through research, interaction design, and usability evaluation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create or contribute to user flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to connect user needs to product outcomes and priorities
- Collaborates with Developers on feasible implementation and design handoffs
- Partners with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility checks where appropriate
- Shares research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager and stakeholders

---

### DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Supports the systems and practices needed to deploy, operate, and monitor the product reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on infrastructure, deployment, reliability, and operational readiness
- Support monitoring, alerting, and production-readiness planning
- Identify operational risks and contribute mitigation plans
- Help define or improve build, deployment, and recovery practices

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers on build, deployment, and operational practices
- Coordinates test environments and operational checks with QA/Testing
- Shares release risks and readiness needs with the Project Manager
- Advises the Product Manager on operational implications of proposed features

---

### Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Helps clarify business processes, requirements, and acceptance criteria so work is understood and verifiable.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document stakeholder needs, workflows, and requirements
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies in proposed requirements
- Help translate needs into clear backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Support requirements traceability and change clarification

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to clarify scope and intended outcomes
- Engages stakeholders to understand needs and confirm shared understanding
- Collaborates with Developers and QA/Testing to make requirements implementable and testable
- Keeps the Project Manager informed about requirement changes that may affect scope or timing

---

### Customer Support / Service Representative

### Role Summary
Brings customer-support experience into planning and helps prepare support teams for changes and releases.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, issues, and feedback themes
- Identify support documentation, training, and readiness needs
- Help prepare customer-facing support information for releases
- Relay post-release feedback and emerging issues to the project team

### Interaction with existing roles
- Shares customer insights with the Product Manager to inform priorities
- Coordinates support readiness and communication needs with the Project Manager
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify reported issues and validate fixes
- Partners with stakeholders on customer-facing updates when appropriate

---

### Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Advises the team on security and privacy requirements, risks, and appropriate review or escalation paths.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements relevant to the project
- Review risks and recommend mitigations or additional assessments
- Advise on secure design, data handling, and validation needs
- Escalate security or privacy concerns through established organizational processes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises the Product Manager and Technical Lead during scoping and design
- Partners with Developers and QA/Testing to incorporate and validate appropriate controls
- Coordinates identified risks and mitigations with the Project Manager
- Works with the designated security response contacts when an incident requires escalation

---

### Cross-team Dependency Owner

### Role Summary
Coordinates a specific deliverable or dependency owned by another team or external partner.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the dependency’s owner, scope, expected delivery, and assumptions
- Track progress and communicate changes or blockers promptly
- Keep dependency information current in the project board and risk register
- Coordinate handoffs and confirm that dependent work is ready to proceed

### Interaction with existing roles
- Reports dependency status and risks to the Project Manager
- Coordinates dates and deliverables with the counterpart team or partner
- Works with the Product Manager when a dependency may affect scope or priority
- Alerts Developers and QA/Testing to changes that affect implementation or validation
